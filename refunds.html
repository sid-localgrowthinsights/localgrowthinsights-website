Refund Policy

When you can get your money back, how to request it, and how fast it processes.

Last updated: April 25, 2026
Effective date: May 1, 2026

Our refund philosophy

If our work doesn't deliver value to you, we'd rather refund you than keep your money. Bad outcomes hurt our reputation more than they help our revenue.

The Audit ($147) — full refund within 7 days

If the audit doesn't tell you anything useful about your practice, reply to the delivery email within 7 days of receiving the report and we'll refund the full $147. No questions, no forms, no "let us address your concerns" calls.

What qualifies as "not useful":

  • The report contained errors about your practice
  • The competitor analysis named the wrong competitors
  • The recommendations were generic and not specific to your situation
  • You learned nothing you didn't already know
  • You're already actively working with an SEO firm and the audit duplicates their work

What doesn't qualify:

  • You disagree with our analysis (that's a different conversation — email us, we'll discuss)
  • You haven't implemented the recommendations yet (we can't refund based on lack of execution)
  • More than 7 days have passed since delivery

GBP Optimization ($247) — partial refund based on completion

The $247 tier includes profile implementation work. Refund eligibility depends on what's been completed:

  • Before work started: 100% refund — $247 returned
  • Work started but not completed: Pro-rated based on work completed
  • Work completed: No refund, but we'll address any specific issues at no additional charge

Review Management ($297/month) — cancel anytime

Cancel at any time. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current paid month. No refunds for partial months already paid. No early cancellation fees.

Local Dominance ($1,497/month) — 3-month minimum then cancel anytime

After the 3-month minimum, cancel at any time. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current paid month. No refunds for partial months. No fees after minimum period.

How to request a refund

For the $147 audit: Reply to the delivery email within 7 days with the words "refund please" — we process it within 24 hours.

For the $247 tier: Email contact@localgrowthinsights.com and we'll respond within 24 hours.

For monthly services: Email us with "Cancel my service" — we confirm same-day and stop billing for the next cycle.

Refund processing

  • Refunds go to the original payment method
  • Stripe processes refunds within 1 business day on our end
  • Funds typically appear within 5-10 business days, depending on your bank
  • You'll receive an email confirmation when the refund is initiated

Contact

For any refund questions: contact@localgrowthinsights.com
