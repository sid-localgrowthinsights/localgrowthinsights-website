Refund Policy

When you can get your money back, how to request it, and how fast it processes.

Last updated: May 12, 2026
Effective date: May 12, 2026

Our refund philosophy

If our work doesn't deliver value to you, we'd rather refund you than keep your money. Bad outcomes hurt our reputation more than they help our revenue. We built this policy to be as straightforward as possible — no grey areas, no gotchas.

Refunds by service

Google Maps Visibility Report — $147

Full refund within 7 days

If the report contains verifiable errors or is clearly not specific to your practice, reply to the delivery email within 7 days and we'll refund the full $147. No forms, no calls, no "let us address your concerns."

What qualifies for a refund:

  • The report contained factual errors about your practice
  • The competitor analysis named the wrong competitors for your area
  • The recommendations were clearly generic and not specific to your practice or city

What does not qualify:

  • You disagree with our analysis or rankings assessment — that's a conversation, not a refund reason. Email us and we'll discuss.
  • You haven't had time to implement the recommendations yet
  • More than 7 days have passed since the report was delivered
  • You found the information useful but decided not to act on it

GBP Audit + Optimization — $247

Pro-rated based on work completed

This service involves active implementation work on your profile. Refund eligibility depends on how much work has been completed at the time of your request.

  • Before work started: 100% refund — full $247 returned
  • Work started but not completed: Pro-rated refund based on tasks completed, determined in good faith between us
  • Work fully completed: No refund — but we will address any specific issues at no additional charge

Review Engine — $297/month

Cancel anytime

Cancel at any time by emailing us with "Cancel my service." Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current paid month. No refunds for partial months already paid. No early cancellation fees, no questions asked.

Starter Website — $297 / Starter Website + GBP — $497

Pro-rated based on completion

Website projects involve active design and development work. Refund eligibility depends on what has been completed at the time of your request.

  • Before work started: 100% refund
  • Work started but not live: Pro-rated based on tasks completed
  • Website live and delivered: No refund — but we will fix any issues at no charge

Local Dominance — $1,497/month

3-month minimum, then cancel anytime

A 3-month minimum commitment applies to allow enough time for meaningful ranking movement. After the 3-month minimum, cancel at any time with 30 days written notice. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current paid month. No refunds for partial months. No fees after the minimum period is complete.

How to request a refund

  • For the $147 report: Reply directly to the delivery email within 7 days with the words "refund please." That's it. We process it within 24 hours.
  • For the $247 optimization or $297/$497 website: Email contact@localgrowthinsights.com with your order details. We respond within 24 hours.
  • For monthly services: Email us with "Cancel my service" and your practice name. We confirm cancellation same-day and stop billing for the next cycle.

Refund processing

  • Refunds are returned to the original payment method used at checkout
  • Paddle processes refunds within 1 business day on our end
  • Funds typically appear within 5–10 business days depending on your bank or card issuer
  • You will receive an email confirmation the moment the refund is initiated
