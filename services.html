What actually happens after you buy.

Most people start with the $97 audit

Roughly 80% of new clients try the audit first. About 1 in 8 then upgrade to the $249 implementation. About 1 in 30 eventually move to Local Dominance. That's normal and expected — the audit is designed to be useful on its own.

We won't push you to upgrade

If you bought the $97 audit and the fixes are working, we'd rather you stay there than feel pressured into a retainer you don't need. The retainer makes sense when GBP becomes a meaningful patient acquisition channel for you, not before.

Local Dominance takes 60-90 days to show results

Google Maps rankings move slowly. Most clients see meaningful position changes by month 2-3, not month 1. If you need new patients in 30 days, you need ads, not GBP optimization.

We turn down clients sometimes

If you're already ranking #1-3 for your main keywords, or already have 200+ reviews, or already work with a dedicated GBP agency, we'll tell you that the audit isn't worth your money. Saving you $97 is worth more than booking a $97 sale.
