Most practices start with the $147 audit

The data tells you exactly what's wrong and what to fix. Many practices implement the changes themselves. Others upgrade to $247 optimization or $297 review management. That's normal and expected.

We won't push you to upgrade

If you bought the $147 audit and the fixes are working, we'd rather you stay there than feel pressured into a retainer you don't need. The retainer makes sense when GBP becomes a meaningful patient acquisition channel for you — not before.

Local Dominance takes 60-90 days to show results

Google Maps rankings move slowly. Most practices see meaningful position changes by month 2-3, not month 1. If you need new patients in 30 days, you need ads — not GBP optimization.

We turn down clients sometimes

If you're already ranking top 3 for your main keywords, or already have 200+ reviews, or already work with a dedicated GBP agency — we'll tell you. Saving you $147 is worth more than booking a $147 sale.
