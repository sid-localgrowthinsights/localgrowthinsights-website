Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information. Written in plain English with zero legalese fluff.

Last updated: April 25, 2026
Effective date: May 1, 2026

What this policy covers

This Privacy Policy describes how Local Growth Insights ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our website (localgrowthinsights.com), our products, or our services. If anything is unclear, email us at contact@localgrowthinsights.com.

What information we collect

Information you provide directly

  • Order information: When you purchase an audit or service, we collect your name, email, practice name, city, and payment information (processed by Stripe — we never store card numbers).
  • Contact form submissions: When you fill out our contact form, we collect the information you provide (name, email, message contents).
  • Free grade tool: When you use our free grade tool, we collect the practice name and city you enter. We do not require an email and we do not store your queries.

Information collected automatically

  • Standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited, timestamps) — kept for 30 days for security purposes
  • Privacy-friendly analytics via Plausible.io — no cookies, no personal identifiers

What we don't collect

  • We don't use Facebook Pixel, Google Analytics, or any cross-site tracking
  • We don't sell or share your information with advertisers
  • We don't track you across other websites

How we use your information

  • To deliver the audits, reports, and services you've purchased
  • To communicate with you about your order or inquiry
  • To send you the case study materials you specifically requested
  • To improve our service quality (in aggregate, never tied to individuals)

Public data about your practice

To produce our reports, we analyze publicly-available information about your dental practice — your Google Business Profile, your competitors' profiles, and Google Maps search results. This information is already public. We don't access any private accounts or proprietary information.

How we protect your information

  • All data transmitted to/from our site uses HTTPS encryption
  • Payment information is processed by Stripe (PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant) and never touches our servers
  • Customer data is stored in encrypted databases with access limited to team members who need it for service delivery
  • We retain order and contact information for 7 years for accounting and legal compliance, then permanently delete it

Your rights

You can request the following at any time by emailing contact@localgrowthinsights.com:

  • Access: Get a copy of all data we hold about you
  • Correction: Update any information we have on file
  • Deletion: Have your data permanently removed (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Portability: Receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Opt-out: Stop all non-essential communications from us

Children's privacy

Our services are intended for businesses, not individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the "last updated" date and notify customers via email.
