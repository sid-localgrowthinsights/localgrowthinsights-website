Methodology

How every audit gets built.

Six steps, applied identically to every practice we work with. The methodology is the product — not the analyst's intuition.

01

Scope & objectives

For each engagement, we identify the practice's core service mix, primary geography, and competitive positioning. This determines which keywords and competitors we benchmark against — no generic dictionaries, no boilerplate.

02

Data collection

Live extraction of the practice's Google Business Profile, top 10 competitors in the local search radius, and the 50 highest-volume dental keywords in the city. All sources are public — Google Maps, Google Search, public review data.

03

14-signal scoring

Each profile is scored across 14 ranking signals Google's local algorithm weights for dental queries: category structure, review velocity, posting cadence, photo freshness, NAP consistency, and 9 others. Red, yellow, green for each.

04

Competitive triangulation

The practice's scores are compared against the top 5 competitors in their local market. We surface gaps the practice can close in 30 days vs gaps that require 60-90 days of compounding effort.

05

Analyst review

A human analyst reviews the automated output, validates the recommendations, and writes the action checklist. Pure automation produces generic reports — we use it as input, not output.

06

Delivery & refresh

The 12-page PDF is delivered within 48 hours of order. For ongoing clients, the methodology runs monthly with a delta report showing what moved, what stuck, and what's next.
