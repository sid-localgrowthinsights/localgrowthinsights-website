Last questions

Things people ask before they order.

Will the report give me anything I couldn't figure out myself?
If you're willing to spend 15-20 hours analyzing your competitors, mapping your keywords, scoring 18+ ranking signals, and synthesizing it into a 30-day plan, yes — you could do this yourself. Most dentists don't have those 20 hours.
What's the difference between this and the $247 GBP Optimization tier?
The $147 audit is the report only — you read it and execute changes yourself. The $247 tier includes implementation: category corrections, business description rewrite, Q&A seeding, 15 photos uploaded, and services section built out — done in 5 days.
Do I need to give you any login info to my Google account?
For the $147 audit: No. We use only public data about your profile. For the $247 optimization tier: Yes, manager-level access (not owner) so we can make changes.
What format is the report in?
PDF, around 8MB. Optimized for both screen reading and printing.

Yes. If you own or work with 3+ practices, email contact@localgrowthinsights.com and we'll set up a multi-practice order at a per-audit discount.
